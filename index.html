FAQ

Before You Decide

Answers to the questions every potential client asks.

How quickly will I see results? ▼

It depends on the service. Paid ads can generate leads within days. SEO and organic social typically show meaningful results in 60-90 days. I'll set realistic expectations during our initial strategy call and provide weekly progress updates so you always know where things stand.

What's the minimum contract length? ▼

I recommend a 3-month minimum to see real traction, but there's no long-term lock-in. After the initial period, it's month-to-month. I earn your business every month — if you're not seeing value, you can cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

Do you work with businesses in my industry? ▼

I've worked across 15+ industries including SaaS, e-commerce, health & wellness, professional services, fintech, and more. Marketing fundamentals are universal — what changes is the strategy, messaging, and channels. I'll research your specific space during discovery.

What do I need to provide to get started? ▼

Access to your social accounts, ad accounts, and analytics. Brand guidelines if you have them (if not, I'll help create them). Beyond that, just your time for a 60-minute kickoff call where we align on goals, audience, and voice. I handle everything else.

How do you measure and report results? ▼

Weekly performance snapshots and monthly deep-dive reports covering all KPIs we agreed on: engagement, reach, leads, conversions, ROAS, and more. Every metric tied back to business impact, not vanity numbers. Plus a monthly strategy call to review and adjust.
