# SocialConnect Vision

## Purpose
This is a portfolio/showcase website for a **digital marketing professional** who provides marketing media services to multiple companies.

## Target User
A digital marketing media individual who:
- Works with multiple clients/companies
- Creates and manages marketing media content
- Needs to showcase their work and attract new clients

## What This Site Should Convey
- Professional credibility and expertise in digital marketing
- A portfolio of work across multiple brands/companies
- Social proof through engagement, testimonials, and case studies
- Modern, polished design that demonstrates marketing taste
- Easy ways for potential clients to connect and reach out

## Design Direction
- Clean, professional, but not boring
- Showcase-oriented — highlight work, results, and client logos
- Strong CTAs for potential clients (contact, hire, learn more)
- Content should feel curated and strategic, not casual
