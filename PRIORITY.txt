PRIORITY: Make the site visually impressive and NOT boring.

The site currently looks generic and basic. Every agent should focus on making it stand out:
- Bolder, more creative visual design — not just clean, but WOW
- Rich, detailed content that tells a story — not placeholder filler
- Interactions that surprise and delight — not just functional hover states
- The portfolio section needs to feel like real work, not colored rectangles
- Typography should have personality — consider a display font for headings
- Add visual depth: layered elements, creative section dividers, asymmetric layouts
- The site should make someone say "I need to hire this person" within 3 seconds

This is a digital marketing professional's site. It should be the BEST example of their own marketing skill.
